How to Submit a Song Request

Follow these steps to tell us about the song you want.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open the Send Request page.
  2. Enter the required details, such as your name and contact information.
  3. Provide the recipient's name, occasion, preferred song style, and your story or message as requested by the form.
  4. Review all details carefully, especially names and spellings.
  5. Complete any payment step displayed on the page, if applicable.
  6. Submit your request and check the confirmation message.
Tip: Include meaningful details about the person, occasion, memories, and mood you want the song to express. Do not include passwords or banking information.

Payment Help

What to do when making a payment for your song.

How to make a payment

  1. Read the price and payment instructions shown on your request page.
  2. Click the payment button provided on the website.
  3. Complete the payment using the available payment option.
  4. Return to your website and follow the instructions shown there.
  5. Keep your payment confirmation or transaction reference for your records.
Only pay using the payment details displayed on your website. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN, or banking password with anyone.

Payment completed, but no confirmation?

  • Check your UPI app or bank statement to confirm the transaction status.
  • Wait for any pending transaction to resolve before attempting another payment.
  • Keep the transaction reference, amount, date, and time.
  • Contact support with these details if you need help.

Avoid making a second payment until you have checked whether the first transaction succeeded.

Song Creation & Delivery

Information about updates and receiving your personalized song.

When will I receive my song?

The creation time depends on your song requirements and the current workload. If a delivery timeframe is provided when you submit your request, refer to that information.

How can I ask for an update?

  1. Keep a copy of your submitted request details.
  2. Contact us through the support details provided on the website.
  3. Include your name, request date, and any available request reference.
  4. Ask for an update on the progress of your song.
If you have not received a delivery estimate, contact support to ask about the expected timeframe.

Changes & Corrections

Need to correct a name or change your song details?

How to request a change

  1. Contact support as soon as you notice an error.
  2. Provide your request details so we can identify the submission.
  3. Clearly explain what needs to be corrected or changed.
  4. Wait for confirmation about whether the change is possible.

Changes may depend on the stage of song creation. Please contact us before assuming that an edit or replacement can be provided.

Privacy & Security

Keep your information safe while using CreateYourSong.

Protect your information

  • Provide only the details needed to submit your song request.
  • Never send your password, OTP, UPI PIN, or banking password through a request or message.
  • Check that payment links and payment details are the ones provided by your website.
  • Do not include sensitive personal information in your song story.
  • Contact support if you are concerned about information you have submitted.
Your website should clearly explain how submitted information is collected, stored, and used. Add a Privacy Policy page that reflects your actual practices.
